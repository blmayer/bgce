    Detach VT keyboard and shortcut children from the console tty.

    KD_GRAPHICS alone still fed keys to the line discipline. Set KDSKBMODE
    K_OFF (restored on exit) so only /dev/input reaches clients, and point
    shortcut children at /dev/null after setsid so new apps are not born on
    the controlling terminal.
