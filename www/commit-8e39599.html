    Store pan in screen pixels so scroll and blit share one lattice.

    world_to_screen/screen_to_world use pan as screen offset; pan by sdx is
    exactly pan -= sdx with fb_scroll. Removes non-invertible world pan and
    zoom math that caused 1px staircase edges and trails when zoomed out.
