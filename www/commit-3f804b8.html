    Integer zoom percent and input-owned software cursor.

    Zoom is discrete 50–400% (step 10, 100 = exact 1:1) with integer
    pan and transforms, so identity blit stays reachable after zooming.
    Software cursor paint is owned by the input thread; compositor paths
    only lift the glyph on damage and mark dirty, avoiding stale 32x32
    underlay squares when the mouse moves over a redrawing window.
