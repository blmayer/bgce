    Pan as integer screen scroll: memmove then fill edges only.

    redraw_pan takes (sdx,sdy) screen pixels, clamps the step at desktop
    bounds, updates pan in lockstep with the scroll lattice, fb_scrolls the
    overlap, and composites only exposed strips. Removes world-pan-first
    math and full-scene clamp fallback that caused 1px edge jogs.
