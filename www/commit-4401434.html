    Fix software cursor trails without a framebuffer mutex.

    Restore underlay from the last paint coords, never paint over an
    existing glyph (that baked pointers into the underlay), and move the
    cursor on every REL/ABS event. Pan/move still lift the cursor before
    scrolling and repaint at the mouse.
